# Optical Endstops

There are 3 optical endstops on the printer. They share one connector labeled "Home Sensor".

## Pinout

| Pin Name  | MCU | Pin Desc | Verified? |
| --------- | --- | -------- | --------- |
| X Endstop | 113 | PD8      | ❌        |
| Y Endstop | 110 | PD9      | ❌        |
| Z Endstop | 117 | PD19     | ❌        |
